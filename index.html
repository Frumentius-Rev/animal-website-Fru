The Ethiopian Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf is Africa's rarest carnivore and lives only in the highlands of Ethiopia nowhere else on Earth! But it's actually the closest living relative of the grey wolf found in Africa. Fewer than 500 are thought to remain in the wild.

  • Habitat: Afroalpine moorlands and grasslands in Ethiopia's highlands, mainly the Bale Mountains and Simien Mountains
  • Diet: Mostly rodents, especially giant mole-rats, hunted alone during the day
  • Conservation status: Endangered - threatened by habitat loss, rabies, and interbreeding with domestic dogs
